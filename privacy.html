Family Dashboard Privacy Policy

What it reads

Family Dashboard reads one household's Google account with read-only permission. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

Where the information goes

The information is stored locally. It is never sold, shared, sent to anyone else, or used for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
