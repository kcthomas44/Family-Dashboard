Family Dashboard Privacy Policy

What it reads

Family Dashboard reads one household's Google account with read-only permission. It reads calendar event titles, times and locations, calendar names and colors, and task titles, due dates and list names. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

Where the information goes

The information is stored only on a computer in the household's home. It is shown on a screen on the home network, and the stored copy is replaced every five minutes. It is never sold, shared, sent to anyone else, or used for advertising or analytics.

How to remove access

The account owner opens their Google Account, goes to Security, then Your connections to third-party apps & services, chooses Family Dashboard, and deletes the connection.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
